Meet Kirti Dhoot

Communication Facilitator, Public Speaking Coach and Trainer.

Kirti Dhoot

I am a Communication Facilitator, Public Speaking Coach and Founder of Script and Speak, an experiential learning initiative that helps learners turn ideas into confident expression.

For the past 5 years, I have worked with 500+ learners across schools, preschools, activity centres and independent programmes, with a focus on children and teenagers aged 6–16, while also working with adult learners and educators.

My approach sits at the intersection of communication, creativity and analytical thinking.

With a postgraduate background in Biochemistry and professional experience in drug discovery, I bring a scientist's curiosity into the classroom: observe, question, experiment, simplify and build.

Meet Manas SM

TEDx Speaker, Published Author, Toastmaster and Leadership Communicator.

Manas SM

Manas SM is a cancer survivor, published author, and TEDx speaker who has turned personal adversity into a platform for inspiring others. A trained Toastmaster, he brings a refined storytelling style to every stage, sharing a message of resilience built on one core belief: never give up.

As Academic Secretary of St. Joseph's College of Commerce (SJCC) and the institution's Best Outgoing Student, Manas has demonstrated leadership grounded in empathy and integrity.

He is also an active voice in sports business and competitive quizzing, with a strong track record across national business fests.

Manas speaks to students, professionals, and emerging leaders on resilience, purpose, and principled leadership — delivering a message that stays consistent wherever he takes the stage: lead with empathy, act with integrity, and always do what's right.
